What changes in the model?

At elasticity 1.2, the model assumes that demand is relatively sensitive to price. This is the report's base-case assumption.

Base case: The report's ₹712 optimum is based on elasticity of 1.2. It should be validated using outlet-level transaction data.
The scenario outputs follow the report's sensitivity analysis and are rounded for display. Lower elasticity means customers are less responsive to price changes; higher elasticity means they are more responsive. These are model scenarios, not actual Barbeque Nation customer-response measurements.

Model benchmark

The base-case mathematical optimum is approximately ₹712.02, producing about 206.1 covers and ₹22,904 daily operating profit. The report identifies ₹699 as a practical price to test because its estimated profit is only about ₹73 per day lower.

How to use this during the presentation

Move the slider from ₹500 to ₹800. Watch the profit curve’s marker move and compare the trade-off between customer volume and contribution. Use the quick buttons to jump to the report’s scenario prices.
